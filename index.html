Why it works

Better legal context leads to more reliable legal outputs.

Learn how to turn your legal knowledge and professional judgment into a Legal Brain: a structured system of interconnected roles, knowledge, memory, instructions and trusted sources that gives AI the context it needs for real legal work.

Instead of rebuilding context for every task or spending excessive time checking unsupported claims, you will learn why AI hallucinates, how to ground its outputs in traceable references, and how to make human verification faster and more focused.

Develop a career-ready Legal AI skill that strengthens your position in a changing legal market and stand out for potential opportunities to join Legal Nodes and work alongside a team at the forefront of AI-enabled legal work.

Please note that participation in the course does not guarantee employment, an interview, or any future role with Legal Nodes, and Legal Nodes is under no obligation to offer employment or other professional opportunities to participants.

The course

What is Legal Context Engineering?

Legal Context Engineering is the flagship course of Legal Brain Academy, an education brand powered by Legal Nodes. It is designed for early-career professionals who already understand the basics of legal work and want a structured, practical approach to using AI.

You will learn how to take a real legal task, structure the knowledge and judgment behind it, connect the right sources, and build a Legal Brain that gives AI the context it needs to support your work.

Why join

  • 01

    Learn how to build the relationships and structures that get AI the information it needs, instead of a simple prompt.

  • 02

    Improve the quality and reliability of AI outputs through better context, instructions and sources.

  • 03

    Turn recurring legal knowledge and tasks into reusable AI workflows.

  • 04

  • 05

  • 06

Your first Legal Brain

What you will build

By the end of the program, you will build the foundations of your first Legal Brain around a selected legal use case.

  1. Role

    What the AI is responsible for, and where human judgment stays essential.

  2. Knowledge

    The legal information and domain context it needs.

  3. Memory

    Historical context that should persist across tasks.

  4. Skills

    Repeatable instructions for recurring legal work.

  5. Sources & tools

    Reliable inputs, verification criteria and connected systems.

Curriculum

Four workshops, one end-to-end use case.

The course is built around engineering a reliable AI workflow for drafting and reviewing contracts. Step by step, you will structure the necessary legal context, configure it in Claude, create reusable Skills and learn how to evaluate and continuously improve AI-generated legal outputs.

Learn what AI needs beyond a prompt to produce relevant, accurate and source-grounded legal outputs.

Turn the Legal Brain into a practical AI workspace. Understand what a Claude Project is, how it works and what elements it contains.

Transform recurring legal tasks into reusable instructions that Claude can execute consistently.

Combine all course components into one working Legal AI system and learn how to improve it through structured evaluation.

By the end of the course, you will have built your own working Legal AI workflow, supported by structured context, trusted sources and clearly defined quality criteria. The workflow is created for your practical use and will not be formally reviewed or graded by the course team.

Audience

Who should apply?

  • Early-career legal professionals.
  • Recent law graduates with practical experience.
  • Candidates curious about Legal AI, automation and the future of legal work.
  • Participants with working English proficiency and basic familiarity with tools such as ChatGPT or Claude.

Please note. The course is not designed for participants with no practical exposure to legal work. Advanced AI expertise or coding experience is not required.
